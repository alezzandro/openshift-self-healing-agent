Title: Use Case 4 — MachineConfigPool Degraded

PANEL 1 — PURPOSE AND GOAL

- Goal: Detect MCPDegraded, explain render failure, open ServiceNow with RCA, generate remediation that removes the conflicting MachineConfig, restore MCP health, and learn for the next run.
- Why it matters: Conflicting MachineConfigs are a real Day-2 problem when multiple teams layer node config; this demo fails at render time before any drain or reboot.

1. Trigger creates MachineConfig self-healing-demo-conflict with Ignition append (rejected by MCO).

4. AAP workflow gathers MCP/MCO diagnostics and creates a ServiceNow incident.

7. Playbook to Gitea; AAP creates Remediate Job Template.
8. Heal: delete conflicting MachineConfig; MCP returns healthy.
9. Store resolution for the learning loop.
