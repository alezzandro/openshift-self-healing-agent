Title: Use Case 2 — Authentication Operator Degraded
Subtitle: Self-healing Day-2 identity configuration on Red Hat OpenShift

Divide the canvas into five clear panels:

PANEL 1 — PURPOSE AND GOAL
- Purpose: Simulate a misconfigured HTPasswd identity provider that references a Secret that does not exist, driving the authentication ClusterOperator to Degraded.
- Goal: Detect ClusterOperatorDegraded, diagnose OAuth/IDP config error, open ServiceNow with RCA, generate remediation, remove the broken IDP, and learn for the next run.
- Why it matters: Identity provider mistakes are common Day-2 operations issues during SSO and IDP changes; the demo shows operator health can fail without taking down existing sessions.

PANEL 2 — HOW IT WORKS
Numbered flow with short lines:
1. Trigger adds a broken HTPasswd IDP pointing at a nonexistent Secret.
2. After operator debounce, authentication ClusterOperator becomes Degraded.
3. Prometheus fires ClusterOperatorDegraded (demo for: ~1 minute); Alertmanager → EDA.
4. AAP workflow gathers diagnostics (operator conditions, OAuth-related context) and creates a ServiceNow incident.
5. Knowledge base lookup in Llama Stack.
6. AI path: OpenShift Lightspeed RAG + LLM (vLLM on Red Hat OpenShift AI) produce RCA and remediation playbook.
7. Playbook to Gitea; AAP creates Remediate Job Template.
8. Heal: remove broken IDP; authentication operator returns healthy.
9. Store resolution for the learning loop.
